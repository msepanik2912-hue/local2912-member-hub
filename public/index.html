Know your rights

Management wants to question you?

You may have the right to request union representation.

Your CBA provides contractual representation rights. Article 20 gives employees who may be subject to discipline the right to request a Union representative during covered interrogations or disciplinary investigations. It also contains specific procedures for disciplinary interviews. Whether a particular meeting is covered depends on the facts.

Read Article 20 in the accessible source reader

Request representation

If you believe the meeting could lead to discipline, you can clearly tell management that you are requesting union representation before continuing the investigatory interview.

I am requesting union representation before I answer questions in an investigatory interview that I reasonably believe could result in discipline. I do not wish to give up that right.

This is suggested plain-language wording, not a required legal formula.

Remember

  • Clearly request a Union representative if you believe questioning may lead to discipline.
  • Article 20 contains additional protections and procedures for disciplinary investigations and interviews.
  • Do not assume every meeting with a supervisor is the same; the facts and purpose of the meeting matter.
  • If you are unsure what kind of meeting this is, contact a steward promptly.

Built for all members

Accessibility

The Member Hub is being designed so that critical union information does not depend solely on sight or solely on hearing.

Blind and low-vision access

  • Semantic HTML, headings and landmarks
  • Full keyboard navigation and visible focus
  • Screen-reader status announcements for dynamic content
  • Text scaling, reflow and strong contrast
  • Accessible HTML contract content rather than PDF-only access

Deaf and hard-of-hearing access

  • Accurate closed captions for video
  • Complete transcripts for video and audio
  • ASL-interpreted versions or interpreter inset for key rights, training and major information where feasible
  • No critical alert communicated only by sound
  • Human review of automated captions before publication

Accessible media standard

When media is added, the interface will expose available alternatives with clear controls such as Captions, Transcript and ASL. ASL will supplement rather than replace captions.
